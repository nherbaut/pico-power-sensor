M20-7820842

8 Pos. Female Receptacle SIL Straight 3mm THT Connector, 8.5mm high, Gold+Tin

2.54mm (0.1") Pitch PCB Connector - M20 Series

  • 2.54mm (.1") pitch female receptacle Single Row straight (vertical) connector, throughboard 3mm PCB terminations - 8 contacts.

  • 8.5mm connector height, top entry. Packaged in tubes.

  • Gold finish on contact area, Tin finish on termination.
