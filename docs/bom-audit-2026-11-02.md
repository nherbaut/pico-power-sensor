## Evidence and limits

- Q1 source: [Vishay product page](https://www.vishay.com/en/product/63242/) and [datasheet 63242](https://www.vishay.com/docs/63242/si4143dy.pdf). The stated 9.2 mΩ at −4.5 V gives a calculated 46 mV and 0.23 W at 5 A using the 25 °C resistance rating, not a warm installed bound.
- Buffer: [TI exact SN74LV125AN page](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LV125A/part-details/SN74LV125AN) explicitly says ACTIVE. This is the PDIP part; the similarly named LVC/other-package variants are not substitutions.
- Holder: [manufacturer OGN sheet, variants table](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/4332306.pdf), page 5, includes exact .G ordering code. This check does not redo populated fit or solder-access review.
- Shunt alternative: [DigiKey France exact MPN](https://www.digikey.fr/en/products/detail/riedon-products-by-bourns/MSR1-0R005F1/1641055). Its distributor Active label does not independently close manufacturer lifecycle.

Complete manufacturer lifecycle requalification for every passive, connector and module was not established. The line-by-line CSV preserves these limitations. SPI/card current, installed losses, fault/thermal behavior, header/HAT fit and the release checklist remain open. ERC, DRC and physical bench tests were not rerun for this documentary review, and historical pass receipts were not refreshed or promoted. None of the sourcing observations is a physical measurement.
