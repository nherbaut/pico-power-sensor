## Pi header: fit, current and approved hole exception

The [Adafruit product page](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2223) explicitly identifies the square-pin change on 2025-07-16. Its linked Denke drawing 241122-05, revision A1, shows 2.54 mm pitch and row spacing, a nominal 51.20 × 5.00 × 8.50 mm body for 2×20 positions, 0.64 ±0.05 mm square tails, an 11.0 ±0.2 mm tail and 1.02 ±0.05 mm recommended PCB holes. The retail description says 10 mm tails. **Resolve that discrepancy and confirm the delivered square-pin revision before creating the final footprint and support heights.** Do not use an older flat-pin drawing or an unrelated catalogue's 28-pin description for this 40-pin part.

The maximum square-tail diagonal from that drawing is calculated as `sqrt(2) × 0.69 = 0.976 mm`. An inherited 1.00 mm nominal finished hole with an assumed ±0.05 mm fabrication tolerance could be only 0.95 mm, smaller than that diagonal. It is not a proven fit. **Owner-approved exception (2026-10-16): all 40 leads of the Adafruit 2223 square-pin Pi stacking socket only: 1.10 mm nominal finished plated holes, ±0.05 mm tolerance, and 2.00 mm pads**. The minimum opening is then 1.05 mm, giving approximately 0.074 mm total diametral allowance against that pin envelope, before positional effects. Actual insertion and fabricator tolerance still require review. The exception approves changing the inherited hole diameter while preserving all centres, outline and mounting holes. **The change is approved but has not been implemented in CAD.**

Samtec SSQ-120-03-G-D uses the previously retained [revision BH assembly print](../hardware/evidence/connectors/samtec-ssq-print.pdf), [recommended layout](../hardware/evidence/connectors/samtec-ssq-footprint.pdf) and [catalogue](../hardware/evidence/connectors/samtec-ssw-ssq-catalog.pdf). Nominal body: 51.31 × 4.95 × 8.51 mm; -03 tail: about 10.01 mm. Its 0.64 mm square tail is a reference dimension; tail sway and row-spacing tolerance need a real insertion check. This alternative remains unselected. Neither the Adafruit-only exception nor earlier approval for the two small Pololu sockets covers this Samtec 40-pin socket; it would need a separate choice and scoped exception.

The socket body goes below the interposer, with long tails passing through the PCB and becoming the upper HAT pins. For a hypothetical 1.6 mm PCB, Samtec's nominal exposed tail is 8.41 mm; Adafruit's drawing gives 9.4 mm, while its retail length would give 8.4 mm. None is an engagement or bottoming guarantee. Select spacers from the actual seated assembly and check Active Cooler, both AI HAT configurations, PCIe ribbon, solder joints and the new protection-module stack. Previous owner-reported hole/header fit is retained; new connector revisions and heights are separate checks.

Adafruit's drawing states **3 A** and **20 mΩ maximum contact resistance**, with no powered-contact count, thermal-rise conditions or aged/hot qualification. At 5 A total, the intended two positive contacts would carry 2.5 A each **only with equal sharing**. In a per-contact 3 A interpretation, the larger positive-contact current must remain at most 3 A; that permits a path-resistance ratio of only 1.5:1. One open contact would leave 5 A in the other, beyond that interpretation. The 3 A interpretation and applicable conditions need confirmation; this is a provisional component direction, not a demonstrated 5 A rating.

For a transparent loss scenario, assign 20 mΩ to each positive contact and each of eight ground contacts and assume equal sharing. The calculated socket-only drop is `5 A × (20 mΩ / 2 + 20 mΩ / 8) = 62.5 mV`, with 0.3125 W total. This is **not a guaranteed installed bound**: the Pi mating pins, temperature, solder joints, current sharing and upper-HAT contacts are not qualified by that substitution. Include Pi and HAT consumption in the total 5 A target, rather than allocating 5 A to each.

Samtec's family catalogue rates SSQ/TSW at 6.3 A per contact with two powered contacts. Its retained power report uses a different long double-row assembly and derates as more contacts are powered. It is stronger family evidence, but neither its 6.3 A number nor Adafruit's 3 A number qualifies the actual Pi/HAT mating stack. An absolute resistance bound for this exact Samtec/Pi pair is still absent.

All 40 contacts remain electrically continuous from Pi to upper HAT. The proposed [40-pin contract](../hardware/review/pi-header-contract.csv) maps each physical pin against Raspberry Pi's [official diagram](../hardware/evidence/pi-harness/raspberrypi-gpio-pinout.png). Pins 2 and 4 receive only the **downstream shunt bus**, and all eight ground contacts join the direct bench return. Pi 3.3 V pins pass through to the HAT; the sole approved interface supply tap is one 10 kΩ Pi-RX pull-up under the scoped [UART-bias exception](uart-interface-options.md). Two 100 kΩ Pi-TX/marker input pull-downs are also included in that exception. Active instrument circuitry remains USB-powered. UART and marker taps require their own qualified interface; do not import the earlier circuit. No alternate Pi USB-C or HAT supply is approved for simultaneous connection.

## Keyed signal cables and assembly

Use two distinct connector widths to distinguish analog sensing from digital communication. JST-XH is **2.50 mm**, not a 2.54 mm pin strip. Four- and five-position housings reduce accidental interchange; they are not a guarantee against forcing or incorrect assembly. Pin-1 labels, cable labels and an electrical continuity check remain required.

The [JST family catalogue](../hardware/evidence/pi-harness/jst-xh-us-catalogue.pdf) specifies 0.90–1.00 mm reference holes for these no-boss headers, with non-cumulative ±0.05 mm centres. Start from **0.95 mm nominal finished holes, ±0.05 mm**, then verify actual fit and eventual fabrication tolerance. The library's 1.7 × 1.95 mm pads violate this project's 1.8 mm minimum; enlarge their copper to **2.00 mm pads** when footprint work is authorized. Bodies are nominally 12.4 × 5.75 mm and 14.9 × 5.75 mm, 7 mm tall; the family mated height is 9.8 mm before cable bend/access allowance. No drill exception is proposed for these headers. Maximum tail dimensions, solder-tip access and full footprint review remain open.

JST's assembly drawing GAM-197, dated 2016-06-23, explicitly lists ASXHSXH22K152, SXH-001T-P0.6 contacts and compatible XHP-4/XHP-5 housings. Length is **152.4 ±5 mm inside, 164.6 ±5 mm overall**, not an exact board-to-board distance. A distributor mirror is retained; the supplier-linked original host returned HTTP406. The larger 203 mm lead variant was found but its exact assembly drawing was not reviewed; it is not silently substituted for a longer reach. Use strain relief and a gentle twist on the two shunt-sense conductors, away from SPI/LED and high-current wiring. Validate usable reach after twisting. The selected leads are black, so mark both ends of every conductor before inserting them. Do not solder wire into crimp contacts or buy loose contacts in addition to the factory-terminated leads.

The [proposed cable contract](../hardware/review/interboard-connector-contract.csv) has four sense conductors and four populated digital conductors, one empty digital cavity, and straight physical pin-number continuity at both ends. Numbers refer to the keyed connectors' eventual verified mating view; they are **not a left-to-right wire-colour assumption**. JST's catalogue PCB view is explicitly from the solder side: mirror it for a component-side footprint about the line through the row centre, then verify pin 1 against the housing/actual part. Number-to-cavity mapping is still unverified; no final footprint was emitted.

**The connector choice does not implement fault protection.** Put suitable fault limiting at the interposer before exposed sense runs; values/topology remain to be selected against monitor bias, noise, settling and accuracy. A sense-to-ground short could otherwise sustain the bench supply's current limit in wiring that is not the DUT power cable. The 3 A family rating with 22 AWG is not a 5 A short-circuit rating. A sense-pair short also invalidates the measured shunt transfer. Bare direct UART wiring is not approved: independently powered Pi and Pico require a circuit that meets both unpowered cases without powering the receiver through an input.

Sense reference and signal ground must not become the intended DUT return. With both cables and USB connected they can form parallel paths; the architecture's reference, ground-current and reversed-bench fault review remains open. Bench off / USB on, USB off / bench on, transitions and reversed/disconnected harnesses remain **unverified**. Assemble and replace cables with both supplies off; no hardware energizing is authorized by this proposal.
