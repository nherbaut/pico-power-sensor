# Mechanical acceptance

Create a 1:1 nonconductive mock-up of the [placement](../../hardware/pcb/interposer/placement.svg) and verify it on the actual Pi 5 and chosen connectors before ordering.

Required checks are listed in [mechanical requirements](../../docs/mechanical.md). Test the Pi with Active Cooler, AI HAT+ and AI HAT+ 2 individually. Both versions require real fit evidence before claiming compatibility; absent equipment means `not tested`.

Document socket orientation and engagement, tail length after the PCB, needed spacers, PCIe cable reach, AI HAT+ 2 heatsink clearance, unobstructed airflow, power-plug/fuse access and side-wing support. Photograph the connector mating and clearances. Cable strain must be reacted by the support rather than the Pi header.

The [paper mock-up photographs](paper-mockup-2026-10-14.md) record partial user-supplied evidence, including outward-wing placement on Pi 3, Pi 4 and Pi 5, user-confirmed 1:1 printing, and Pi 5 cooler/header-stack views. Exact connector engagement, pin-1 mapping, 1.6 mm PCB seating, standoff alignment, component-to-cooler clearance and both HAT configurations remain unverified. The Pi appears powered in its photos; further fitting must be done unpowered.

The Pi 3 standoff photo adds preliminary mounting-location evidence. The paper-placement stage is sufficient for preliminary review; further paper-only photographs are unnecessary. The full mechanical gate remains partial because the remaining checks require rigid geometry, exact connectors and the intended cooler/HAT stack.
